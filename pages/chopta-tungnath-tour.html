Chopta Tungnath Overview

✨ Customized packages are also available as per your travel dates, budget and requirements.

Chopta, often called the 'Mini Switzerland of Uttarakhand', offers one of the most scenic treks in the Indian Himalayas. The Tungnath trek leads to the highest Shiva temple in the world, surrounded by panoramic views of Nanda Devi, Trishul, and Chaukhamba peaks. This 4-day package combines spirituality, adventure, and natural beauty.

Experience meadows, rhododendron forests, and a summit climb to Chandrashila for a sunrise you will never forget. Perfect for beginners and seasoned trekkers alike.

Key Highlights - Chopta Trek

Tungnath Temple

Highest Shiva temple in the world (3,680m), steeped in mythology and panoramic grandeur.

Chandrashila Summit

Sunrise view from 4,000m – a 360° Himalayan panorama including Nanda Devi and Trishul.

Deoria Tal & Forests

Enchanting lake reflection and dense rhododendron / oak forests along the route.

Small Group Experience

Max 15 travelers, experienced trek leader, safety equipment, and personal attention.

✨ Detailed Itinerary

Day 1
Rishikesh/Haridwar – Chopta (Drive approx 180 km / 7 hrs)

Start early from Rishikesh, drive along the Ganga and through Devprayag, Rudraprayag and Ukhimath. Reach Chopta by evening, check into alpine camps / guest house. Leisure walk in the meadows. Overnight under starlit sky.

Day 2
Chopta – Tungnath – Chandrashila – Chopta (5 km trek one way)

Wake up to a Himalayan sunrise. Start trek to Tungnath temple (3.5 km) through rhododendron forest. Explore the ancient shrine, then climb further to Chandrashila summit (1.5 km) for a 360° view of Nanda Devi, Trishul, Kedar and Chaukhamba. Descend back to Chopta for hot dinner and rest.

Day 3
Chopta – Deoria Tal – Sari Village (4 km trek) – Chopta

After breakfast, drive to Sari village and trek to Deoria Tal, a pristine lake reflecting Chaukhamba peak. Enjoy packed lunch by the lake, then return and drive back to Chopta. Evening bonfire with music and stories.

Day 4
Chopta – Rishikesh/Haridwar departure

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast, optional short nature walk. Depart by 10 AM for Haridwar/Rishikesh with beautiful memories. Drop at your preferred location.
