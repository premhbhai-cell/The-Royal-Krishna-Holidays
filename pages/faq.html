Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our Char Dham Yatra packages and services

Booking Questions

How do I book a Char Dham Yatra package?

You can book a Char Dham Yatra package through our website, by calling us at +91 9555188688, or by WhatsApp. Simply choose your preferred package, select your travel dates, and provide the number of travelers. Our team will confirm your booking and guide you through the payment process.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes, we recommend booking at least 2-3 weeks in advance, especially during peak season (May-June and September-October). This ensures better accommodation options and availability of preferred travel dates.

Can I customize my package?

Absolutely! We offer fully customizable packages. You can modify the itinerary, add or remove destinations, choose different accommodation types, or extend your stay. Contact our team to discuss your preferences.

Payment Questions

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, UPI payments, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and cash payments at our office. For international bookings, we also accept credit/debit cards.

Is there a booking advance required?

Yes, we require a booking advance of 50% of the total package cost to confirm your reservation. The remaining balance can be paid before or on the day of departure.

Are there any hidden charges?

No, we believe in complete transparency. All costs are clearly mentioned in the package details. There are no hidden charges. Any additional services you opt for will be communicated upfront.

Cancellation Policy

What is your cancellation policy?

Our cancellation policy is as follows: 100% refund if cancelled 30+ days before departure, 75% refund if cancelled 15-29 days before, 50% refund if cancelled 7-14 days before, and no refund if cancelled less than 7 days before departure.

How long does it take to process a refund?

Refunds are typically processed within 7-10 business days after the cancellation request is approved. The amount will be credited to the original payment method.

Travel Questions

What is the best time for Char Dham Yatra?

The Char Dham Yatra typically opens in late April/early May and closes in October/November. The best time to visit is May-June and September-October when the weather is pleasant. The monsoon months (July-August) can be challenging due to landslides.

What is the fitness level required for Char Dham Yatra?

The yatra involves moderate trekking, especially to Kedarnath (16km trek) and Yamunotri (6km trek). A basic level of fitness is recommended. Elderly pilgrims can opt for pony/palanquin services available at all dhams.

Do you provide transportation during the yatra?

Yes, all our packages include comfortable transportation (Tempo Traveler/SUV/Bus depending on group size) for the entire duration of the yatra. The vehicles are well-maintained and driven by experienced drivers familiar with mountain routes.

Accommodation Questions

What type of accommodation is provided?

We provide clean and comfortable hotel/guest house accommodation. Depending on the package, you can choose from budget, standard, or premium options. All accommodations are selected for their cleanliness, safety, and proximity to the temples.

Are meals included in the package?

Yes, most of our packages include breakfast and dinner. Some premium packages include all meals. Pure vegetarian meals are provided throughout the yatra, keeping in mind the spiritual nature of the journey.

General Questions

Do you provide helicopter services for Kedarnath?

Yes, we can arrange helicopter tickets for Kedarnath Yatra. Please note that helicopter tickets are subject to availability and weather conditions. We recommend booking helicopter services well in advance.

Is it safe for solo female travelers?

Yes, Char Dham Yatra is safe for solo female travelers. Our experienced staff ensures the safety and comfort of all travelers. We can also arrange for female co-travelers if preferred.

Do you arrange group tours?

Yes, we regularly organize group tours for Char Dham Yatra. Group tours are a great way to meet fellow pilgrims and enjoy the journey together. Contact us for upcoming group departure dates.
