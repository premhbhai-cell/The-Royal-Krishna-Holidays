Manali Kasol Solang Sissu Overview

✨ Customized packages are also available as per your travel dates, budget and requirements.

Escape to the breathtaking valleys of Himachal Pradesh! This 6-day itinerary is a perfect blend of adventure, relaxation, and scenic beauty. From the vibrant hippie culture of Kasol and the thrilling activities in Solang Valley to the tranquil beauty of Sissu and the bustling charm of Manali, this journey promises memories for a lifetime.

Drive through the iconic Atal Tunnel, stroll by the Parvati River, and witness the majestic snow-capped peaks. Ideal for friends, couples, and families looking to experience the best of Himachal.

Key Highlights - Himachal Adventure

Solang Valley Activities

Enjoy paragliding, zorbing, ropeway rides, and snow adventures (seasonal) in the adventure capital of Himachal.

Kasol & Parvati Valley

Walk through pine forests, visit the charming village of Kasol, and soak in the bohemian vibes by the Parvati river.

Atal Tunnel & Sissu

Drive through the world's longest highway tunnel to reach the pristine Sissu Valley, a hidden gem with stunning waterfalls and lake.

Manali Sightseeing

Visit Hadimba Temple, Old Manali cafes, Mall Road, and Vashisht hot springs for a complete local experience.

✨ Detailed Itinerary

Day 1
Delhi/Chandigarh – Manali (Overnight Journey)

Board the comfortable Volvo/AC coach from Delhi/Chandigarh in the evening. Enjoy the overnight scenic journey through the hills of Himachal towards Manali.

Day 2
Manali Arrival & Local Sightseeing

Reach Manali in the morning, check into the hotel. After freshening up, visit the famous Hadimba Devi Temple, Club House, Tibetan Monastery, and Vashisht village known for its hot springs. Evening free to explore Mall Road and Old Manali cafes. Dinner and overnight stay in Manali.

Day 3
Solang Valley Excursion

After breakfast, drive to the majestic Solang Valley. Spend the day indulging in adventure activities like paragliding, zorbing, ATV rides, and ropeway (at your own expense). In winter, enjoy snow activities. Return to Manali by evening. Overnight stay.

Day 4
Manali – Kasol – Manikaran

Check out from Manali and drive to the 'Amsterdam of India' – Kasol. Walk along the Parvati River, explore the local Israeli cafes and market. Later, visit the sacred Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara and hot springs. Drive back to Manali or stay in Kasol (depending on package). Overnight stay.

Day 5
Atal Tunnel – Sissu Excursion

Early morning departure towards the Atal Tunnel. Drive through the 9.02 km engineering marvel to reach the gorgeous Sissu Valley. Visit Sissu Lake, Waterfall, and enjoy the panoramic mountain views. Return to Manali in the evening. Farewell dinner.

Day 6
Manali – Delhi/Chandigarh Departure

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast at the hotel. Check out and spend some free time on Mall Road for last-minute shopping. Board the evening Volvo for Delhi/Chandigarh with sweet memories of the trip.
