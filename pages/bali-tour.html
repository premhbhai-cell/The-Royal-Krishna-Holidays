Bali Tour Overview

✨ Customized packages are also available as per your travel dates, budget and requirements.

Bali, the famed 'Island of the Gods', is an Indonesian paradise that blends stunning natural beauty with a unique culture. Explore emerald rice terraces, sacred temples like Tanah Lot and Uluwatu, and relax on world-class beaches. This 6-night/7-day package offers the perfect mix of adventure, spirituality, and relaxation.

From the artistic vibes of Ubud to the vibrant nightlife of Kuta and Seminyak, experience the best of Bali with comfortable stays, private transport, and guided sightseeing.

Pristine Beaches

Relax on the golden sands of Kuta, Seminyak, and Nusa Dua with crystal-clear waters.

Temples & Culture

Visit iconic sea temples like Tanah Lot and Uluwatu, perched on dramatic cliffs.

Ubud & Rice Terraces

Wander through the artistic heart of Bali and the stunning Tegalalang rice terraces.

✨ Detailed Itinerary

Day 1
Arrival in Bali – Kuta / Seminyak

Welcome to Bali! Upon arrival at Ngurah Rai International Airport, our representative will greet you and transfer you to your hotel in Kuta or Seminyak. Spend the rest of the day at leisure, explore local markets, or relax by the pool. Overnight stay in Bali.

Day 2
Ubud Art Village & Kintamani Volcano View

After breakfast, proceed to Ubud. Visit Celuk (silver), Mas (wood carving), and Batuan (painting) villages. Continue to Kintamani for a breathtaking view of Mount Batur volcano and Lake Batur. Enjoy lunch overlooking the crater, then visit Tegalalang rice terraces and Ubud Monkey Forest. Return to hotel.

Day 3
Bedugul & Tanah Lot Sunset Tour

Drive to the highlands of Bedugul to visit the iconic Ulun Danu Beratan Temple, situated on Lake Beratan. Explore the local fruit and flower market. In the afternoon, head to Tanah Lot, a stunning sea temple perched on a rocky outcrop. Witness a magical sunset behind the temple. Overnight stay.

Day 4
Water Sports & Uluwatu Kecak Dance

Morning is free for optional water sports at Tanjung Benoa (banana boat, jet ski, parasailing – extra cost). Later, visit Garuda Wisnu Kencana (GWK) cultural park. In the evening, drive to Uluwatu Temple, perched on a cliff 70 meters above the sea. Watch the spectacular Kecak fire dance during sunset. Return to hotel.

Day 5
Nusa Penida Island Excursion (West)

Early morning transfer to Sanur harbor for a speedboat ride to Nusa Penida. Explore the famous Kelingking Beach, Angel's Billabong, Broken Beach, and Crystal Bay. Enjoy a day amidst dramatic cliffs and turquoise waters. Return to Bali by evening and check back into hotel.

Day 6
Tegenungan Waterfall & Leisure / Spa

After breakfast, visit the beautiful Tegenungan Waterfall near Ubud. Enjoy the lush greenery and a refreshing dip. Spend the rest of the day at leisure for shopping in Seminyak or indulging in a traditional Balinese spa (optional). Farewell dinner at a beachside restaurant.

Day 7
Departure from Bali

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast at the hotel. Depending on your flight time, you may have time for last-minute souvenir shopping. Our representative will transfer you to the airport for your onward journey, taking back beautiful memories of Bali.
