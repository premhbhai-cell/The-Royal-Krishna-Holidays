10 Essential Tips for Kailash Mansarovar Yatra

Home / Blog / 10 Essential Tips for Kailash Mansarovar Yatra

Jan 10, 2026   Admin

Prepare yourself for a sacred, high-altitude journey that is comfortable and safe

The Kailash Mansarovar Yatra is one of the most demanding and rewarding pilgrimages. It involves high altitude, changing weather and long travel days, so careful preparation makes a real difference.

  1. Get a medical check-up early and discuss altitude with your doctor.
  2. Start a fitness routine weeks ahead, with walking, stairs and breathing exercises.
  3. Keep your passport valid and organise permits well in advance.
  4. Pack in layers: thermals, fleece, windproof outer shell, gloves and a warm cap.
  5. Carry sunscreen, sunglasses and lip balm, as the sun at altitude is intense.
  6. Drink plenty of water and follow your guide's acclimatisation schedule.
  7. Avoid rushing, as slow and steady walking conserves energy.
  8. Bring a small first-aid kit with your regular medicines.
  9. Travel with a reliable operator who handles logistics, safety and support.
  10. Keep an open mind: weather can change plans, so build flexibility into your schedule.
