Do Dham Yatra Ex Delhi Overview

Embark on a divine journey to the sacred shrines of Kedarnath and Badrinath with our meticulously planned 5 Nights / 6 Days Do Dham Yatra from Delhi. This package ensures a comfortable and seamless pilgrimage, covering the two most revered destinations in the majestic Himalayas.

Our expert team handles all logistics, from comfortable accommodations in Haridwar, Guptkashi, Kedarnath, and Badrinath to reliable transportation and experienced guides. Whether you seek spiritual solace or wish to witness the breathtaking beauty of Uttarakhand, this yatra is designed to provide an unforgettable experience.

Key Highlights - Do Dham Yatra

Kedarnath Dham

One of the 12 Jyotirlingas, dedicated to Lord Shiva, located at an altitude of 3,583 meters amidst stunning Himalayan peaks.

Badrinath Dham

Dedicated to Lord Vishnu, situated between Nar and Narayan mountain ranges near the holy Alaknanda River.

Itinerary

Arrive in Delhi and meet our representative. Drive to Haridwar, the gateway to the Do Dham Yatra. On arrival, check into the hotel. In the evening, witness the mesmerizing Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri. Dinner and overnight stay in Haridwar.

After breakfast, drive to Guptkashi via Tehri Dam. Enjoy the scenic drive along the Mandakini river. Arrive and check into the hotel. Dinner and overnight stay in Guptkashi.

Early morning drive to Sonprayag. Start the trek to Kedarnath. You can also opt for a helicopter or pony ride (at your own cost). Visit the Kedarnath temple in the evening. Overnight stay at Kedarnath (basic accommodation).

After morning darshan, trek down to Sonprayag. Drive back to Guptkashi and then continue towards Badrinath via Joshimath. Check into the hotel upon arrival. Dinner and overnight stay in Badrinath.

After morning darshan at Badrinath, visit Mana Village (the last Indian village). Later, drive to Rudraprayag / Srinagar. Dinner and overnight stay.

After breakfast, drive back to Delhi. Arrive in Delhi by evening. The tour ends with divine blessings and cherished memories.

Travel Essentials

Things to Carry for Do Dham Yatra

Pack smart and travel light for a comfortable and safe pilgrimage experience

Documents & ID
  • Valid Government ID (Aadhar/Passport)
  • Passport size photographs
  • Medical certificate & prescriptions
  • Booking confirmation copies
  • Emergency contact list
Clothing & Footwear
  • Warm woolen clothes & thermals
  • Raincoat / waterproof jacket
  • Comfortable trekking shoes
  • Extra pairs of socks
  • Woolen caps, gloves & muffler
Medicines & First Aid
  • Personal prescribed medicines
  • Pain relievers & fever medicine
  • Band-aids, antiseptic cream
  • ORS packets & glucose
  • Altitude sickness medicine
Personal Items & Misc
  • Sunscreen lotion & lip balm
  • Torch / flashlight with batteries
  • Power bank & charging cables
  • Water bottle & dry snacks
  • Walking stick for trek
Pro Travel Tip

Carry light luggage (max 10kg). Weather in the mountains changes rapidly, so always be prepared for rain and cold. Keep essential items in a small daypack for easy access during treks.
