Thailand Highlights: Bangkok & Phuket

Experience the perfect blend of culture, nightlife, and island serenity on this 6-day Thailand adventure. From the bustling streets and ornate temples of Bangkok to the pristine beaches and limestone cliffs of Phuket, this tour is designed for unforgettable memories. Explore floating markets, indulge in Thai cuisine, and set sail to the stunning Phi Phi Islands or James Bond Island.

Perfect for couples, families, and friends, this package includes comfortable stays, guided city tours, island excursions, and enough leisure time to soak in the tropical vibes. Get ready for the Land of Smiles! 🏝️✨

Key Highlights - Thailand Experience

Bangkok City & Temples

Explore the Grand Palace, Wat Pho, and vibrant markets. Dive into the chaos and charm of Khao San Road.

Phi Phi Island Tour

Speedboat ride to Maya Bay, snorkeling in crystal clear waters, and a beachside lunch in paradise.

Phuket Beach Life

Patong, Karon, or Kata – relax on soft sands, enjoy water sports, and witness spectacular sunsets.

Thai Street Food & Culture

Savor Pad Thai, Mango Sticky Rice, Tom Yum, and explore night markets and cabaret shows.

✨ Detailed Itinerary

✨ Customized packages are also available as per your travel dates, budget and requirements.

Day 1
Arrival in Bangkok – Transfer to Hotel & Evening Market

Upon arrival at Suvarnabhumi Airport, our representative will greet you and transfer to your hotel. Check-in and relax. In the evening, explore the vibrant streets, visit a nearby night market, and try authentic Thai street food. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 2
Bangkok City & Temple Tour – Grand Palace & Wat Pho

After breakfast, enjoy a guided tour of Bangkok's most iconic landmarks. Visit the Grand Palace, the Temple of the Emerald Buddha, and Wat Pho with the giant reclining Buddha. Later, enjoy a long-tail boat ride through the canals. Evening at leisure to explore Khao San Road or a rooftop bar. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 3
Fly to Phuket – Leisure evening at Patong Beach

After breakfast, transfer to Bangkok airport for a short flight to Phuket. Upon arrival, check into your resort and unwind. Spend the evening strolling along Patong Beach, witness a beautiful sunset, and enjoy the lively nightlife and local seafood. Overnight in Phuket.

Day 4
Phi Phi Island Tour by Speedboat (with lunch)

Today, embark on a full-day excursion to the famous Phi Phi Islands. Cruise by speedboat, visit Maya Bay (where "The Beach" was filmed), snorkel in Pileh Lagoon, and enjoy a buffet lunch on Phi Phi Don. Visit Viking Cave and Monkey Beach before returning to Phuket. Overnight in Phuket.

Day 5
James Bond Island & Phang Nga Bay Tour

After breakfast, set out for another iconic adventure. Cruise through the emerald waters of Phang Nga Bay, visit the famous James Bond Island (Khao Phing Kan), explore sea caves by canoe, and enjoy a seafood lunch. Return to Phuket for a relaxed evening. Overnight in Phuket.

Day 6
Departure from Phuket – Fly back home

Enjoy a final Thai breakfast at the resort. Depending on your flight schedule, transfer to Phuket International Airport for your journey back home with wonderful memories of Thailand. 🌴
