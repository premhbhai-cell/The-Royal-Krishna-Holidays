Udaipur, often called the 'Venice of the East' and the 'City of Lakes', is a jewel of Rajasthan. This 4-day package immerses you in royal heritage, stunning palaces, and serene boat rides on Lake Pichola. Explore the majestic City Palace, wander through vibrant bazaars, and witness breathtaking sunsets from Sajjangarh Fort.

Perfect for families, couples, and history enthusiasts, this tour offers a blend of cultural immersion, architectural marvels, and relaxed leisure time in one of India's most romantic cities.

Key Highlights - Udaipur Heritage

City Palace & Heritage

A majestic complex on the banks of Lake Pichola, offering intricate Rajput and Mughal architecture.

Lake Pichola Boat Ride

Cruise through the serene waters, visiting Jag Mandir and witnessing panoramic ghat views.

Heritage Walks & Bazaars

Stroll through old city lanes, colorful markets, and experience the local Rajasthani culture.

Scenic Viewpoints

Enjoy sunset views from Sajjangarh Monsoon Palace and panoramic cityscapes from Karni Mata Temple.

✨ Detailed Itinerary

Day 1
Arrival in Udaipur & Evening Heritage Walk

Upon arrival at Udaipur railway station/airport, transfer to your hotel. After some rest, embark on a guided heritage walk through the old city. Explore the vibrant bazaars, temples, and havelis. In the evening, enjoy a traditional Rajasthani dinner with cultural performance. Overnight stay in Udaipur.

Day 2
City Palace, Vintage Car Museum & Lake Pichola Boat Ride

After breakfast, visit the magnificent City Palace complex, a blend of Rajasthani and Mughal architecture. Explore the Crystal Gallery and Vintage Car Collection. Later, proceed to Lake Pichola for a serene boat ride to Jag Mandir. Spend the evening at leisure near Gangaur Ghat. Overnight stay in Udaipur.

Day 3
Fateh Sagar, Saheliyon ki Bari & Sunset at Sajjangarh

Begin the day with a visit to the beautiful Saheliyon ki Bari (Garden of Maidens) and the serene Fateh Sagar Lake. Optionally visit Shilpgram crafts village. In the evening, drive to Sajjangarh Monsoon Palace for a spectacular sunset view over the city and lakes. Return to hotel for dinner.

Day 4
Eklingji & Nagda Temples – Departure

After breakfast, check out and visit the ancient Eklingji Temple complex and the historic Nagda Temples (about 22 km from Udaipur). Explore the intricate carvings and serene atmosphere. Later, transfer to Udaipur airport/railway station for your onward journey with wonderful memories.
