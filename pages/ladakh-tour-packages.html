Leh Ladakh Overview

Ladakh, often called the 'Land of High Passes', offers one of the most dramatic landscapes in India. This 11-day adventure takes you through the world's highest motorable passes, serene high-altitude lakes like Pangong and Tso Moriri, and ancient monasteries. From the magnetic hills to the sand dunes of Nubra Valley, every moment is filled with awe.

Experience the stark, breathtaking beauty of the cold desert, ride through Khardung La, and witness a sunset that paints the mountains in hues of gold and purple. Perfect for adventure lovers, photographers, and anyone seeking the road trip of a lifetime.

Key Highlights - Ladakh Adventure

Khardung La Pass

One of the world's highest motorable passes (5,359m). A thrilling gateway to Nubra Valley.

Pangong Tso Lake

The iconic blue lake at 4,350m, changing colors from azure to deep blue. Camping under the stars.

Nubra Valley & Hunder

Cold desert with sand dunes, Bactrian camels, and the confluence of Shyok and Nubra rivers.

Monasteries & Culture

Visit Hemis, Thiksey, and Shey monasteries. Explore Leh Palace and experience Ladakhi culture.

✨ Detailed Itinerary

Day 1
Arrival in Leh | Acclimatization Day

Arrive at Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport, transfer to hotel. Complete rest and hydration is crucial for acclimatization. Evening leisurely walk to Leh Market and Shanti Stupa. Overnight at hotel in Leh.

Day 2
Leh Local Sightseeing & Monastery Tour

After breakfast, visit Shey Palace, Thiksey Monastery, and Hemis Monastery. Witness the stunning architecture and ancient relics. Return to Leh by evening. Explore local cafes and bazaars.

Day 3
Leh to Nubra Valley via Khardung La

After breakfast, drive to Nubra Valley over the legendary Khardung La (5,359m). Descend into the cold desert, visit Diskit Monastery and the huge Maitreya Buddha statue. Evening at Hunder sand dunes for a camel ride (optional). Overnight in Nubra Valley.

Day 4
Nubra Valley to Leh | Turtuk Excursion (optional)

Day trip to Turtuk village near the LOC (if permitted) or explore Sumur and Panamik hot springs. Later drive back to Leh. Evening at leisure. Overnight in Leh.

Day 5
Leh to Pangong Lake via Chang La

After breakfast, drive to Pangong Tso through Chang La (5,360m). Witness the mesmerizing blue lake. Check into camps near the lake, enjoy the sunset and starry night. Overnight at Pangong.

Day 6
Pangong Lake to Tso Moriri

Early morning sunrise at Pangong. After breakfast, drive to Tso Moriri via Chushul and Nyoma (offbeat route, subject to permits). Reach the serene high-altitude lake, check into camps. Overnight at Tso Moriri.

Day 7
Tso Moriri to Leh via Tso Kar

Explore Tso Moriri and the nearby Korzok monastery. Drive back to Leh via Tso Kar and the More Plains. Arrive in Leh by evening. Overnight in Leh.

Day 8
Leh to Sham Valley | Hall of Fame & Magnetic Hill

Day excursion to Sham Valley. Visit Hall of Fame, Gurudwara Pathar Sahib, Magnetic Hill and the confluence at Nimmu. Return to Leh. Overnight in Leh.

Day 9
Leh to Lamayuru & Uleytokpo

Drive to Lamayuru, one of the oldest monasteries in Ladakh, known for its moon-like landscape. Visit Alchi monastery on the way back. Overnight at Uleytokpo or return to Leh (depending on comfort).

Day 10
Leh Free Day | Optional Rafting / Cycling

Day at leisure in Leh. Explore local markets, try Ladakhi cuisine, or opt for white water rafting on the Zanskar river (additional cost). Evening farewell dinner. Overnight in Leh.

Day 11
Departure from Leh

After breakfast, transfer to Leh airport for your onward journey. Take home unforgettable memories of the high passes, blue lakes, and ancient culture.
