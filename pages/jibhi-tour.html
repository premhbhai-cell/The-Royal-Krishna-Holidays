Jibhi Overview

✨ Customized packages are also available as per your travel dates, budget and requirements.

Nestled in the Tirthan Valley of Himachal Pradesh, Jibhi is an offbeat paradise for nature lovers and solitude seekers. Often described as a 'hidden gem', it offers dense pine forests, gurgling streams, and traditional wooden architecture. This 5-day package allows you to unwind with cozy homestays, gentle nature walks, and the soothing sound of the Tirthan river.

Perfect for families, couples, and solo travelers, the itinerary balances relaxation with soft exploration of nearby waterfalls, lakes, and quaint villages.

Key Highlights - Jibhi Escape

Tirthan Valley Walks

Stroll through scenic trails, cross wooden bridges, and breathe in the fresh mountain air.

Jalori Pass & Serolsar Lake

Drive to the 3,120m high pass and trek through oak forests to a pristine alpine lake.

Cozy Wooden Stays

Experience warm Himachali hospitality in traditional pinewood cottages and riverside camps.

River & Angling

Relax by the Tirthan river; known for trout fishing (seasonal permits) and picnics.

✨ Detailed Itinerary

Day 1
Arrival in Jibhi | Leisure Day

Arrive from Delhi/Chandigarh by overnight Volvo or private cab. Check into your cozy wooden cottage. Spend the evening walking around the tiny hamlet, crossing the Jibhi waterfall bridge, and unwinding by the riverside. Overnight at Jibhi.

Day 2
Jibhi – Jalori Pass – Serolsar Lake – Jibhi

After breakfast, drive to Jalori Pass (approx 1 hr). Enjoy panoramic Himalayan views, then trek 5 km through dense forest to Serolsar Lake. Have a packed lunch by the lake, return to Jibhi in the evening for a bonfire with music and local snacks.

Day 3
Chehni Kothi & Shringa Rishi Temple

Post breakfast, hike to the ancient Chehni Kothi tower (approx 2 hrs one way), a 1500-year-old architectural marvel. Visit Shringa Rishi Temple in Banjar valley, known for its local deity significance. Return to Jibhi for a relaxed evening and dinner.

Day 4
Raghupur Fort & Meadows

Trek through pine and deodar forests to the ruins of Raghupur Fort. Soak in the views of the valley and enjoy a picnic lunch in the meadows. Return by evening, rest, or explore the local market for souvenirs. Overnight at Jibhi.

Day 5
Departure from Jibhi

Enjoy a final breakfast amidst nature. Check out and proceed to your onward destination with unforgettable memories of the hidden gem of Himachal.
