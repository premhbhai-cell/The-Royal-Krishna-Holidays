By making a booking with Divine Earth Holidays, you confirm that all information provided is accurate and complete. All bookings are subject to availability and confirmation by our team.

  • Any special requests (dietary, medical, accessibility) must be communicated at the time of booking.
  • Divine Earth Holidays reserves the right to modify or cancel any booking due to unforeseen circumstances.

2 Payment Terms

The following payment terms apply to all bookings:

  • Advance Payment: 50% of the total package cost is required to confirm your booking.
  • Balance Payment: The remaining amount must be paid at least 15 days before the departure date.
  • Last-Minute Bookings: Full payment is required for bookings made within 7 days of departure.
  • Payment Methods: We accept bank transfers, UPI, and major credit/debit cards.

3 Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellation charges are calculated on the total tour price:

  • 30+ days before departure: 25% cancellation fee
  • 15-29 days before departure: 50% cancellation fee
  • 7-14 days before departure: 75% cancellation fee
  • Less than 7 days before departure: 100% cancellation fee (No refund)

Refunds, if applicable, will be processed within 7-10 business days via the original payment method.
